Brahma Sutras (Shankaracharya)

by George Thibaut | 1890 | 203,611 words

Hinduism Hindu Philosophy Vedanta
Buy relevant books

Summary: English translation of the Brahma sutras (aka. Vedanta Sutras) with commentary by Shankaracharya (Shankara Bhashya): One of the three canonical texts of the Vedanta school of Hindu philosophy. The Brahma sutra is the exposition of the philosophy of the Upanishads. It is an attempt to systematise the various strands of the Upanishads which form the background of the orthodox systems of thought.
